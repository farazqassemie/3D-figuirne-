Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and deletes information when you use this website to turn photos into a 3D model or figurine (the “Service”). It includes our Biometric Data Notice and Retention Schedule and a description of your privacy rights.

The short version: you upload photos so we can make your 3D model. Photos are sent to our 3D-generation service providers for that purpose only. We do not sell or share your information for advertising, we don’t use tracking or advertising cookies, and we delete photos after days and 3D models after days. You can delete everything sooner from your model’s page.

2. How we use information

  • To make the 3D model you asked for, show it to you, and produce and deliver your figurine if you place an order.
  • To run, secure, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the Service (for example, checking why a generation failed).
  • To keep records of your consent and to comply with the law, respond to legal requests, and enforce our Terms.

We do not use your photos or 3D model to identify you, for facial recognition, for advertising or profiling, or to train our own AI models. We do not use your sensitive information to infer characteristics about you.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts or terms that limit how they may use it:

  • 3D generation providers. Your photos are sent to third-party AI 3D-generation services, which create the 3D model. Depending on the option you choose, a provider may be located in, or process data in, countries outside the United States, including China (including Hong Kong). These providers may keep copies under their own retention practices.
  • Payment provider, to process your payment and prevent fraud. It handles your card or bank details under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and infrastructure. Our website, files and logs are hosted by a cloud hosting provider in the United States.
  • 3D viewer library. The model viewer on the results page is loaded from a public content delivery network, which receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads.
  • Production and shipping partners, only if you place an order, to make and deliver your figurine.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, or to protect rights, safety and property, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (subject to this policy).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are defined in California law). We have not done so in the past 12 months.

4. Biometric Data Notice and Retention Schedule

This section is our written, publicly available policy for biometric identifiers and biometric information, including under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, Washington law and similar laws.

  • What we collect. Photos of a person’s face and body that you upload, and the 3D model generated from them, which may include scans or measurements of face geometry.
  • Why. Only to make the 3D model and figurine you asked for, and to deliver it to you. We do not use it to identify anyone.
  • Consent first. We collect and process this data only after you give written consent on the upload page, before any photo is processed. If the photos show someone else, you must have that person’s consent (or a parent’s or legal guardian’s consent for a minor) before you upload.
  • No sale or profit. We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric data.
  • Disclosure. We disclose it only to the service providers described in Section 3, to complete your request, or where required by law.
  • Security. We store it using reasonable security measures that are at least as protective as how we store other confidential information. Each job’s files can be opened only with that job’s private link.

5. Retention of other information

Consent and order records are kept for as long as needed to show we obtained consent and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations (generally up to 7 years). Hosting logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider.

6. Cookies and tracking

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies or tracking pixels. The site only uses what’s needed to make it work. Because we don’t sell or share data, there is nothing to opt out of. We still treat Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out request.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Iowa, Delaware, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Nebraska, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island and other states), you may have the right to:

  • Know and access the personal information we have about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Opt out of sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling. We don’t do these.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We only use it to provide the Service.
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.
  • Appeal our decision on your request by replying to our response. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General.

How to make a request

  • Fastest: open your model’s page (the link you got after uploading) and click “Delete my photos and model”. This deletes your photos and model from our servers immediately.
  • By email: . Include your model’s link or job code so we can find your data. We don’t ask for your name or account, so the link is how we verify the request is yours.

We respond within 45 days (we may extend this once by 45 days if needed, and we’ll tell you). You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that they are authorized.

8. Children

The Service is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or legal guardian may upload photos of their own minor child to order a figurine, and doing so gives the consents on the upload page on the child’s behalf. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), private, unguessable links for each job, and access controls on admin tools. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

10. International processing

We operate in the United States. As described in Section 3, some service providers may process your photos outside the United States, including in China (including Hong Kong), where data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

We’ll post any changes here and update the date at the top. If we make a material change to how we use biometric or other sensitive information, we’ll ask for your consent again before applying it to you.
