Making: Model 1 · figurinechange

6 photos, same framing in every one. This is what makes your model look like you.

For the best likeness

  • Frame from the waist up — same distance in every shot (don't step closer for some)
  • Look straight at the camera on the front angles — eyes open, head level
  • Soft even light — face a window, plain wall behind you
  • No close-ups mixed with far shots — inconsistent framing breaks the model
  • Take off watch, glasses, and hat
  • Don't look down, away, or tilt your head
  • JPEG or PNG only — iPhone HEIC photos aren’t supported; export/share them as JPEG first
